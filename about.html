Our History

R.K. Desai College is an educational institution located in Vapi, Gujarat. Our fictional college history describes an institution dedicated to learning, knowledge and student development.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide quality education, develop practical skills and encourage students to become responsible professionals.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a supportive learning environment where students can explore their interests and achieve their goals.

College Facilities

1. Library

The library provides books and study materials to support students in their studies.

2. Computer Laboratory

The computer laboratory allows students to practise computer applications and programming.

3. Sports Facilities

Sports activities encourage teamwork, discipline and physical fitness.

4. Student Support

Teachers provide academic guidance and help students understand their subjects.
